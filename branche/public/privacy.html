Your information on Branche

Private preview notice · Updated November 10, 2026

Branche is operated by Kreischtech Contractors LLC, doing business as KreischTech. For privacy, recovery or safety questions, contact kreischtech@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

We store your account name, email, password hash and sign-in sessions to run your account. We store business profiles, service requests, community posts, uploaded images, messages, reports and blocking preferences to provide those features and investigate abuse. Request information may be used for rate limits and hosting security logs.

Public and private information

Published business pages, open requests, community updates and their attached images are visible to visitors. Your login email and password hash are not shown in the directory. Messages are visible to conversation participants; authorized operators can review reported conversations. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Unattached images and images on unpublished content are accessible only to their owner through the app.

Hosting and outside services

Vercel hosts the app and uploaded images. Neon hosts account and community records. Google Gmail sends recovery emails when configured. These providers process information needed to run the service. Website and video links open external sites under those sites’ policies; videos are not automatically embedded or played. We do not sell your personal data.

Photos and logos

Uploaded photos are resized and re-encoded, removing embedded metadata such as GPS tags. Faces, addresses and other details visible in a photo are not automatically removed. Upload only content you have permission to share. You can detach images or delete unused uploads in your image library.

ContractorSight connection

An optional one-use connection code links your ContractorSight business identity to Branche. This shares the business name and account identifiers. It does not transfer projects, financial records, photos, messages, subscription benefits or login sessions. You can disconnect it from the ContractorSight tools page in Branche.

Retention and deletion

Active account and community records remain until you delete the relevant content or account. In your account menu, choose Delete Branche account and confirm your password. This removes your Branche profile, posts, images, requests, connections and conversations from active storage, including shared conversations. ContractorSight is unaffected. Provider backups and security logs may retain copies until their configured retention expires. Contact us if deletion cannot be completed or you need help accessing your information.

Cookies and choices

Branche uses a necessary sign-in cookie. This private preview does not load advertising trackers or Firebase Analytics. You may browse published content without a Branche account, subject to preview access restrictions. You can unpublish your business page, remove your posts, close requests, block users and report concerns.

Preview limits

This is still a private preview. Operator access and email delivery must be configured before public launch. Do not use sensitive customer information while testing. For immediate danger, contact emergency services rather than relying on an app report.
