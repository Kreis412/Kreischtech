About this test

Branche by KreischTech is a local marketplace test operated by Kreischtech Contractors LLC, doing business as KreischTech. Accounts here are separate from ContractorSight. Basic pages, job posts and replies are free in this test. No money is collected and no paid service is created by posting or messaging. This is not ready for public customer use; use test information.

Business information

Business names, contact people, services, license numbers and insurance information are entered by the business. They have not been independently verified. Do not claim verification you have not received. Whether a license is required depends on the service and jurisdiction; an optional field does not waive a legal requirement. Do not upload government ID, tax IDs, policy documents or other sensitive records.

Respect people and their privacy

No impersonation, fraudulent offers, harassment, discriminatory abuse, spam or misleading credentials. Do not post exact home addresses, access codes, private phone numbers or someone else’s personal information in the public feed. Share only information you have permission to share. Business pages and open job posts can be seen by other test visitors. Login emails are not shown in public listings.

Messages, blocking and reports

Conversations are visible to their participants. Authorized operators may access stored records to administer the service or investigate abuse reports. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. You can block another account to stop new messages and report a page, post or conversation. Reports are saved for operator review; this test does not promise immediate or 24-hour moderation. For immediate danger, contact emergency services.

Before agreeing to a job

Compare written estimates, check references and agree on scope, materials, timing and payment milestones. Customers and businesses make their own work agreements. Branche does not currently handle deposits, escrow, insurance, background checks, guarantees or dispute resolution for the work itself. These terms do not remove applicable consumer rights.

Your test data

The test stores account names and emails, password hashes, session tokens, profiles, job posts, conversations, reports and blocking preferences. Public pages are opt-in. You can unpublish a business page or close a job request. For test-account deletion or a privacy concern, contact kreischtech@gmail.com. A public launch requires a complete privacy notice, retention rules and account-deletion flow; this test is not a substitute for those.

Practical guidance

Read the FTC’s home-improvement scam guidance. This notice is a safety reminder, not a claim that scams cannot occur.
