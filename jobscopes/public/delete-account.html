ContractorSight · Privacy

Request account and data deletion

ContractorSight is provided by Kreischtech Contractors LLC, doing business as KreischTech. You can request deletion without signing in or reinstalling the app.

Send your request

Email kreischtech@gmail.com with the subject ContractorSight account deletion. Include your account email and company name. Say whether you also want your company workspace deleted. Do not send your password, pilot code, payment card details or API keys.

Email a deletion request

This opens your email app; you must send the email to submit the request. If no email app opens, send the request directly to the address above. Support verifies your identity and authority before deleting records and will confirm the scope and completion with you.

What is deleted

For a verified account deletion, we remove your sign-in account, password hash, sessions and company memberships. For a verified company-owner request to delete the entire workspace, we also remove its project records, uploaded photos and documents, measurements, estimates, equipment records, messages and saved AI results from active application storage.

If you belong to a shared company, its business records may need to remain for the other authorized members. We review any personal information in those records with the company owner. Deleting your account does not authorize deletion of another person's company workspace.

Subscriptions and retained records

Deleting or uninstalling an app does not automatically cancel a subscription. Include any cancellation request in your email. For a Google Play subscription, you can also cancel in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. For a website subscription, use ContractorSight's Plan & usage settings or contact support.

We may retain billing, refund, fraud-prevention and legally required records for the applicable legal or dispute-resolution period. Copies in backups and service-provider systems can have separate retention periods. We explain any applicable exceptions and expected deletion timing when responding to your request. Account deletion is not merely deactivation.

Files on your devices

Downloaded reports, backups and photos you saved separately remain on your devices. Remove those copies yourself if needed. Offline photos may remain in browser storage; clear ContractorSight site data on each device after saving anything you want to keep.
