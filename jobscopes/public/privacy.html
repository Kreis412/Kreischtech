Privacy notice

Effective October 22, 2026 · Kreischtech Contractors LLC, doing business as KreischTech. Contact: kreischtech@gmail.com.

Information we process

We process your account name, email, company, password hash, project records, uploaded photos and documents, measurements, estimates, equipment information, messages, AI results and usage records. Uploaded files may contain embedded metadata. Payment identifiers, subscription status, billing consent and account activity records help us provide access, resolve billing issues and protect the service. Hosting providers may also process technical request information.

How it is used and shared

We use this information to run ContractorSight, save and synchronize your work, provide requested AI features, manage subscriptions, prevent abuse and provide support. Render hosts the application and stored records. Stripe processes card payments; ContractorSight does not store full card numbers. Support correspondence sent to our Gmail address is processed by Google.

Business owners can save a profession and specialties for their company. These self-reported fields are included in new Joe requests and photo analyses, including when project context is off. Owners can clear these fields in Settings. When you request cloud photo analysis, the selected image and relevant analysis context are sent to OpenAI. Joe sends your question and up to three recent exchanges. Selected project context is included when you enable it; financial records and chat photos are not automatically included. We request that API responses are not stored as retrievable responses, but this does not eliminate provider safety retention. See OpenAI's API data controls for its retention and data-use practices.

Company members can access records according to their role. Existing team workspaces give managers editing access and viewers reading access, including financial records. Share access only with people you authorize. We do not sell your project information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate abuse and protect the service.

Customer progress links and messages

When a contractor publishes a customer page, its chosen updates, progress, dates, milestones and selected original photos are available to anyone possessing that private link. Original photos may contain embedded metadata. Customer pages do not automatically include internal financial records or other projects. Link holders can send messages labeled Customer; this does not verify their identity. Contractors and managers can read and reply to the conversation. Messages are stored with the project sharing records until the project is deleted by an owner or manager, or they are removed through a verified data-deletion request. Links expire after 90 days and can be replaced or revoked by the contractor. Revocation stops future access; it cannot remove copies already saved by recipients. Do not share the link publicly. Messages are not automatically sent to AI. Email and push notifications are not currently provided.

Owners and managers may also publish a company profile and up to six selected past-job photos with captions on all active customer pages. This is optional and hidden by default. Publishing a gallery shares those selected images across customer links, including when they came from other projects. Removing a photo or disabling the profile stops future gallery access but cannot recall copies already saved.

Storage and security

Sessions use cookies. Browser storage holds preferences and may hold queued offline photos on your device. An offline queue is not a server backup. Use a trusted device and secure it. Hosted connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and workspace access is checked on the server. These protections do not make data end-to-end encrypted or eliminate all security risks.

Appointment requests

When you request an appointment, we store your name, email, optional phone number, job address, description and requested time for the business owner and managers to review. The public booking link shows business availability, not customer details. Your private status link shows the appointment time and approval status to anyone holding it. Appointment requests are not automatically sent to AI, and automatic email or text notifications are not provided. Contact the contractor or our support address to request correction or deletion of appointment information; we verify identity and authority before acting.

Retention and your choices

Canceling a subscription does not automatically delete saved work. Owners and managers can delete a project and its associated photos, estimates, work records, Joe conversations and customer portal messages in the app. Recorded company transactions and audit history are retained, and existing backups are not erased by this action. Contact us to request account or project-data access, correction or export. To delete your account and associated data, use our account deletion request page; sign-in is not required. We verify your identity and authority over company records before acting. We retain records needed to operate your workspace and may retain billing, security or legally required records after a deletion request. Backups and service providers can have separate retention periods.

You can choose not to use AI features and can turn off Joe in Settings. Do not upload sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your job. ContractorSight is intended for business use, not for children. Contact us about privacy questions or applicable privacy rights.

Optional local weather and daily check-in

When you choose Use my location, your browser asks permission for device location. Approximate coordinates (rounded to two decimal places) are sent through our server to MET Norway to retrieve a forecast. Forecasts and approximate coordinates are cached temporarily in server memory, not saved to your account. MET Norway receives our server IP address, not your device IP. We do not track your location in the background. Personal check-in tasks and preferences are stored in this browser for your account; clear browser storage to remove them.

Optional morning notifications

Notifications are off unless you enable them and allow your browser or phone permission. We store your device push subscription, chosen reminder time and time zone to send one daily reminder through your browser’s push provider. Notifications contain no financial amounts or task details. Turn them off in Settings → Daily check-in. Signing out also turns off reminders on that device.

Optional usage analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics for Firebase collects general feature-use events, browser and device information, a pseudonymous browser identifier, and approximate geographic information inferred by Google from your connection. This helps us understand which features are used and whether actions succeed. Analytics is off until you choose Allow analytics. We do not send photos, notes, project names, email addresses, financial amounts or precise device location to Analytics. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

You can withdraw permission in Settings - Privacy & analytics on each browser or device. Withdrawal stops future collection and removes our Google Analytics cookies on that browser; it does not automatically erase previously collected data. Analytics preferences are stored locally. See Google's privacy policy and contact kreischtech@gmail.com with questions or deletion requests.
