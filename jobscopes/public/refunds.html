Refund policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · Kreischtech Contractors LLC, doing business as KreischTech.

Request a full refund within 7 days of your first subscription payment. This applies even if you have used part of your allowance. Later requests, including renewal-payment requests, are reviewed individually. Your applicable statutory rights remain unchanged.

How to request a refund

Email kreischtech@gmail.com with your account email and payment date. We may ask for information to verify the purchase. Never send your password, API keys or full card details. Approved refunds go back through the original payment method; bank processing times vary.

Stop future charges

In Settings → Plan & usage, choose Cancel renewal at period end, or ask us to cancel renewal in your refund request. A refund by itself does not cancel a subscription. Refunded or disputed payments can remove unused AI allowances; previously saved project records are not deleted by a refund.
