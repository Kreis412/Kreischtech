What AgentOS actually does on your machine

Everything you see in this demo is read-only. Here's what happens when you own it and press start.

AI Lead Discovery

Click ⚡ AI Discover 5 Leads and Agent 2 queries your local Ollama model to generate realistic, enriched contractor prospects for any city and trade you choose — complete with estimated revenue, contact info, and financial leakage scores. Results save directly to your local SQLite database.

1-Page AI Audit Reports

For every lead, Agent 3 generates a custom Foreman Agent Value Audit — a full one-page brief showing the contractor exactly how much revenue they're losing from missed job-site calls. Written by Ollama llama3.1, zero API cost, tailored to their trade and city.

Autonomous Email Outreach

Agent 4 drafts a personalized B2B cold email for each lead and sends it via your Gmail SMTP — with strict deduplication so no one ever gets emailed twice. Runs in dry-run review mode by default so you approve every message before it goes live.

Hot Lead Phone Alerts

When a contractor replies, Agent 5 fires an instant Windows desktop toast notification, an SMS to your phone via carrier gateway, and generates a ready-to-read 2-minute call script with the contractor's numbers already filled in.

Import Your Own Leads

Drop any CSV from Google Maps, Apollo.io, or LinkedIn Sales Navigator into the Import Real Leads modal. AgentOS parses it, runs financial audits on every row, and loads them into your pipeline instantly — ready for outreach.

1-Click Launch, Runs Forever

Double-click start_agentos.bat and all 5 agents boot simultaneously in the background. No terminal knowledge needed. The dashboard opens in your browser automatically. Press stop anytime — your data is always saved locally, nothing in the cloud.
